# Backward compatibility, version migration and innovation: exact prior-art screen
Date: 2026-11-09 JST. Status: direct publisher HTML and original abstracts verified, NOT a systematic exhaustive review.

## Why R12 was chosen
R12 (standards revision/legacy upgrade investment) from research/research_gap_discovery.md was prioritized because an actual standards organization made both backward-incompatible and compatible version upgrades. ISO-style votes and the rejected JPET design equations cannot simply be inserted here.

## THE crucial closest theoretical original results
1. Kristiansen, E. G. (1998), "R&D in the Presence of Network Externalities: Timing and Compatibility," RAND Journal of Economics 29(3):531–547. Original journal abstract https://ideas.repec.org/a/rje/randje/v29y1998iautumnp531-547.html . **Economic result ALREADY known:** Two firms choose timing of R&D and compatibility; network externalities can induce excessively early incompatible innovation; common standards before introduction can slow introduction; a standardization policy optimal ex post may be undesirable ex ante. Published full proof restricted through JSTOR in this inspection; do NOT certify theorem-level non-inclusion.
2. "The timing of product introduction and the credibility of compatibility decisions," *International Journal of Industrial Organization* 14(6) (1996):801–823, https://doi.org/10.1016/0167-7187(95)01001-7 . Original journal ABSTRACT: compatibility timing can reverse private incentives and create time inconsistency; welfare optimal international and domestic standardization studied. Full text not retrieved; exact propositions not verified.
3. Farrell & Saloner (1986), "Installed Base and Compatibility: Innovation, Product Preannouncements, and Predation," AER 76:940–955, https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/faculty-research/publications/installed-base-compatibility . Installed base can create excess inertia or excess momentum during gradual transitions. Already known.
4. Kende (1994), "A note on backward compatibility," Economics Letters, https://doi.org/10.1016/0165-1765(94)90042-6 . Original abstract: hardware/software system innovator may optimally abandon backward compatibility; no entitlement to claim the compatibility/innovation tension is new.
5. Aoki & Arai (2013/2015 WP), "Evolution of Standards and Innovation", RIETI 13-E-075 (https://www.rieti.go.jp/en/publications/summary/13090005.html) and 15-E-136 (https://www.rieti.go.jp/en/publications/summary/15120003.html). Incumbent/consortium invests to improve existing standard, enlarge installed base, deter entry; joint incumbent and entrant innovation also studied. No novelty from generic incumbent investment to protect an old standard.
6. Wang & Buccella **(2023, not 2024)** "The Timing of Technology Adoption in Network Industries," *Review of Industrial Organization* 62:367–392, https://doi.org/10.1007/s11151-023-09902-4 . **Directly examined original open-access publisher HTML and Proposition 1–4:** Proposition 1 network strength threshold and product substitution can delay leader/follower adoption; Proposition 2 social vs private early/late adoption under rational expectations, Proposition 3 competitive and network adoption timing under output commitments, Proposition 4 welfare-optimal timing versus market-led adoption. The new model should NOT claim that network effects accelerate or delay adoption as a generic new discovery.
7. Berends, Deken & Hummel (2026), "Dynamic platform strategies in an interconnected ecosystem: Navigating standards and complements in the emerging smart home ecosystem," *Research Policy* 55(10):105609, early online **2026-09-11**, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.respol.2026.105609 . **Full original publisher HTML sections 1, 4–7 inspected.** This is a qualitative/longitudinal **EMPIRICAL** study of 97 smart-home platforms and four modes using proprietary/third-party standards and complements, finding increased combination rather than replacement. It does **not** present a named analytical theorem or closed-form equilibrium. Its findings strongly constrain claims about first-party and third-party standards/multimoding. DO NOT invent "Proposition 2" of this paper.
8. Schott, Schaefer & Bekkers (2026), "Chinese firms bridging the standardization gap in 3GPP: liabilities of outsidership and the role of technological shifts", *Journal of Economic Geography*, published 2026-02-05, https://doi.org/10.1093/jeg/lbaf064 . Directly inspected publisher HTML. The authors' **Hypotheses 1–3** predict Chinese firms' collaboration network reach during 3G→4G. These are **empirical hypotheses**, not game-theoretic propositions. 3G/4G technology shifts provide observed relevance but cannot be sold as an undiscovered phenomenon.
9. Doganoglu & Wright (2006), "Multihoming and compatibility," *IJIO* 24:45–67, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijindorg.2005.07.004 . Multihoming may weaken competition and create social inefficiency; R11 multi-standard layering needs more than its existence.
10. OCA primary https://openchargealliance.org/faq/ and https://openchargealliance.org/protocols/open-charge-point-protocol/ : OCPP 1.6↔2.0.1 NOT backward-compatible; OCPP 2.1 designed to preserve 2.0.1 application logic. 2.0.1 edition 3 was also approved as IEC 63584 in 2024. This is an **industry consortium technology decision**, not a one-country-one-vote ISO governance decision.

## Critical exclusion
No general scholarly novelty from "revision timing induces investment delay" or "backward compatibility and installed base shift incentives" — directly anticipated by 1986, 1996, 1998, 2013, and 2023 work. The 2026 Research Policy article is not a theoretical theorem to refute, but empirical constraints on platform capability/mode choices.

## Narrow research diagnostic
A minimal two-firm complementary-investment game under externally chosen backward-compatibility intensity b was solved exactly. It yields an explicit region where frontier investment is U-shaped rather than decreasing in backward compatibility, driven by sufficiently strong cross-version investment complementarity and legacy market scale. That algebra is verified, but its mechanism likely follows familiar strategic-complementarity investment models and DOES NOT include an endogenized committee vote, prices, output, or a full welfare objective. **Economic originality N2 maximum pending exact proof-level audit**, NOT an independent paper headline.

## Precise precedence tests that matter most
1. Spulber (2019) Proposition 6: no SEPs, endogenous ex-post competitive group equilibrium, majority choice efficient under his assumptions. **Not** “all SSO votes are efficient”; importing his welfare conclusion into national ballots would be false. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/ecoj.12606
2. Barrett & Yang (2001): proposition numbering referenced here is of revised 1999 IATRC working paper ONLY, not checked for numbering in final published JIE. https://ageconsearch.umn.edu/record/14597/files/wp9909.pdf
3. Zhao et al. (2007): publisher original abstract expressly integrates standard development & adoption with network effects and heterogeneous developer, passive adopter, nonadopter statuses; published *full proofs not obtained*. https://www.jmis-web.org/articles/1061
4. Ott et al. (2026): publisher open HTML establishes standard-setting adversarial feature dilution; no claim it proves the exact voluntary OCPP parameter cutoff. https://doi.org/10.1093/joclec/nhag023
5. OECD 2026 is institutional descriptive evidence, not a new game-theory theorem. https://doi.org/10.1787/56f2cfc7-en

## Non-containment falsification gates for new priority P8
Compare with Gandal & Shy (2001), Costinot (2008), Klimenko (2009), Wallner (1998 WP), Schmidt & Steingress (2022), OECD 2016 and 2026, and WTO CAP empirical research. Check whether “more harmonized text -> higher conformity testing barrier” is merely strategic non-tariff substitution. Hold conformity policy fixed; hold technical harmonization fixed; remove foreign/local certificate recognition asymmetry; remove domestic profit lobbying. If the sign pattern persists identically after removing the institutional interaction, the theory is not independent.

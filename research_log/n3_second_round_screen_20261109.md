## Strongly relevant (but not an N3 theorem)
WDR 2025 explicitly recommends tiered international standards adjusted to capacities. ISO/IEC 2026 Directives Annex G calls for essential market differences and justified optional pathways, and prioritizes performance-based standards. These are important institutional tensions about shared technical vocabulary vs different performance floors. Still cannot claim that policy recommendations or their tradeoffs are not covered by quality ladders, minimum-standard and firm-investment literature.

## What an actual N3 result would need
1. Original peer-reviewed theorem comparators with precise equations, not mere paper-title similarity.
2. Market prices/entry/trade/innovation welfare result that **cannot** be reproduced by old quality ladder, lock-in, membership or pooling mechanism.
3. Economic primitives supported by an actual standards organization and verified measurement of a commercial dependent variable.
4. Nonempty open parameter proof, counterexamples, and ablations of the new institution.
5. If any prerequisite fails, do not claim N3; no lower-standard proposition will be presented as main result.

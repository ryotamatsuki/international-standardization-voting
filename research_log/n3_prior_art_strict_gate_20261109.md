## Directly inspected scholarly primary publications
1. Stadler, Tobler Trexler & Unsorg (2022), *The Perpetual Trouble with Network Products Why IT Firms Choose Partial Compatibility*, *Networks and Spatial Economics* 22:903–913. Full open publisher HTML https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11067-022-09572-x . They derive an **explicit** symmetric endogenous compatibility choice followed by Hotelling price competition; partial compatibility under coordination costs; common standard-setting decision in §4; their k*=min{(5+4b)β/(12γ),1} in their assumed setting. This covers partial interoperability, network strength, investment costs, firm rents and prices. Distinct central-reference test topology not analyzed in this text, but this absence does NOT certify originality.
2. Huang, Tan, Teh & Zhou (2024), *Network Interoperability and Platform Competition*, NET Institute WP 24-03, https://ideas.repec.org/p/net/wpaper/2403.html . Their accessible abstract says **general pairwise, coalition and industry-wide interoperability matrix** and price competition among n symmetric platforms; network-structure × strength × nonlinearity determine equilibrium prices. Paper is a working paper (not conclusive peer-review) and full proof not directly examined in this audit. **Direct collision** with generic “hub-spoke structure changes price competition” narrative.
3. DeMarzo, Kremer & Skrzypacz (2019), *Test Design and Minimum Standards*, *American Economic Review* 109(6):2173–2207, abstract/publisher https://benny.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.20171722; author PDF https://web.stanford.edu/~skrz/Test_Design.pdf. ## N3 threshold policy for future proposed research
**Do not present a new main proposition unless ALL gate tests have documented results.**
G1: Read closest at least 3 original full texts (or clearly specify that failure to access prevents certification), record exact propositions and assumptions with precise locations.
G2: Distinguish genuinely novel economic mechanism/outcome from generic crowding out, coordination costs, network topology, complementarity and mathematical thresholds known before; provide constructive nested baseline and counterexample to simple relabeling.
G3: Present a formally proved result on a nonempty OPEN region, with correct equilibrium, existence, price/entry/welfare and no ad hoc sign chosen by target payoff or sharp policy choice.
G4: At least two economic ablations remove the claimed effect; verify crucial new institution is independently causal. Where it survives a deletion, downgrade significance and originality.
G5: Show economically meaningful prediction of prices, entry, welfare, cross-border trade or innovation, provide measurable observables and falsifiers linked to a real standardization/testing institution.
G6: Check robustness to the main natural competing formulations (e.g., cost functional forms, test topology, market competition, heterogeneous firms, deviations and legal feasibility).
G7: Conclude N3 only if exact prior theorem noncontainment, original mechanism, material economic impact and proof jointly pass. If original source inaccessible or novelty contested, **report NOT VERIFIED and do not present it as novel proposition**.

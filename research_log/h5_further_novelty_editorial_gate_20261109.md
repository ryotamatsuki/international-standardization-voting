## Candidate editor's sharp questions
1. Product market missing: can the assumed concave U(n) be derived from actual Cournot/Bertrand, network effects, and national ownership rather than imposed as a reduced-form loss? In particular, a standard linear Cournot model with one domestic and one foreign producer can have a convex import-cost welfare response, so the required sign is not automatic.
2. Institution missing: do importing countries genuinely face an indivisible 'recognize foreign reports / retest locally' menu, or is accredited equivalence, statistical sampling, provisional acceptance, partial MR, post-market checks and risk-tiered testing available? OECD 2016 documents many intermediate modes.
3. Legal concern: WTO TBT Articles 5.1.1–5.1.2 constrain de facto discrimination and unnecessary barriers. An authority cannot simply choose protectionist tests unconstrained in a normative model of lawful regulation.
4. Prior-art collision: is this only Maggi–Mrázová WP fixed cost discontinuity + Wallner selective protection + OECD mode choice, with the policy-choice result following automatically? If yes reject novelty.
5. Scope ambiguity: the model concerns a national import conformity policy, **not** an ISO national-body standards vote; it cannot be advertised as an extension of Gandal & Shy's ISO stage without further evidence.
6. Empirics: technical harmonization tends to be negotiated with MRA itself; a clean independent harmonization shock plus subsequent switch in retesting is needed for identifying the causal mechanism.

## Referee 1: industrial organization
MAJOR CONCERN: Gross operating return s(b+theta*a) is reduced form. No Cournot/Salop/Hotelling price equilibrium and no firm-specific endogenous portfolio design; what is called "interoperability" is defined as s*a² rather than modeled from product-pair functionality. This risks a new notation for an ordinary adoption elasticity. REQUIRE: price and product-market continuation and predictions that depend on competition.

## Referee 2: international economics
MAJOR CONCERN: Nothing in the proof is international. National P-member standards institutions, cross-border export exposure, and foreign profit ownership are absent. Even adding national payoff weights is elementary Gandal-Shy accounting unless it creates a novel non-additive interaction. REQUIRE: cross-country participation or market-access prediction not explainable by generic compliance cost models, and meaningful government/standards-body agency.

## Referee 3: standards institutions
MAJOR CONCERN: OCPP certification is industry-consortium rather than an ISO national vote. ISO approval, national adoption, Core certification, optional profile certification, and actual interoperation are different objects. OCA optional ISO15118 flag helps empirical relevance but no ISO ballot is observed. REQUIRE: precise scope of an SSO rule actually affecting a real implementation decision, no conflation with fictional government mandates.

## Referee 4: game theory
The exact cutoff fixed point, welfare derivatives and general F density elasticity are algebraically sound within reduced-form payoffs. But firms' returns s(b+theta*a) already abstract out all strategic market choices. For V=theta=0 upper welfare branch is flat; unique welfare maximum requires V+theta>0 (corrected in proof). Potential equilibrium selection under heterogeneous distributions requires local stability condition 1-theta*f(z)/s>0. Need solve the proposed richer game rather than assume it.

Do not promise publication in IJIO/Journal of Economics or assert probabilities without a real new prediction.

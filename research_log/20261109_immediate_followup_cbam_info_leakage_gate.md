## 4. Independent math falsification: third-country exporter response
Melitz-Pareto market shares S_ij=(D_ij*phi_min^(sigma−1)/F_ij)^(k/(sigma−1)).
In market-separable case dS_ik/dF_ij=0. This identity was symbolically verified in Python/SymPy.
With an ARBITRARILY posited coupling F_ik_effective=F_ik+alpha*F_ij, d log S_ik/dF_ij=−alpha*k/((sigma−1)*(F_ik+alpha*F_ij)) <0, but that is **mechanically assumed via alpha**, not economically derived.
**Adversarial no-go:** negative third-country effects or positive third-country spillovers from an arbitrary joint fixed-cost term cannot be an N3 theorem. Only supply-chain or inspection-lab endogeneity justified by a distinct welfare/entry model could possibly matter.

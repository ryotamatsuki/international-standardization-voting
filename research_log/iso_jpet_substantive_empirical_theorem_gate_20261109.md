# Substantive empirical-theory proposition screen: ISO institution x firms x market
**Date:** 2026-11-09 (JST). **Decision:** PRIOR ART FIRST; no certified new N3/N4 proposition yet.

## 1. Research target / baseline separation

- Model A: rejected JPET manuscript, 3 countries/3 firms, Cournot, x_i endogenous *residual* characteristics after government compatibility g, national welfare CS plus resident firm's worldwide profits. The \(\mu_N=D-\mathcal L\) result already exists. Direct grand agreement acceptance despite inherited-depth rejection already exists. Do not label either a new theorem.
- Model B: Gandal & Shy (2001) international recognition and trade, not an ISO committee.
- Model C: linked ISO vote research repository, 4 countries Salop product market, country vote and voluntary firm adoption; old compatible-brand and new KS current-user versions have different pricing and welfare functions.
- The goal is NOT to create a hybrid model of all three. Choose a single minimal set of primitives only after novelty screen.

## 2. Discarded as published/main propositions

1. More network effects cause stronger resistance to standardization: Barrett–Yang (2001), Klimenko (2009), Cheng–Huang (2025), and repo K-S prior-art audit. Network-intensity reversal needs more than the sign.
2. Equal ISO votes, asymmetric populations, welfare-improving standard rejected: general voting/accounting plus Goerke–Holler (1995), Spulber (2019); repo already shows large K/q generalization remains elementary quota composition.
3. Unanimity under-adopts, majority over-adopts: generic vote/social-externality wedge already known; previous P2 required special damages; the security microfoundation has a close 2026 cybersecurity/compatibility publication.
4. ISO publication vs firm adoption gap: Barrett–Yang (2001), Zhao–Xia–Shaw (2007), broader adoption review. More stages alone cannot be claimed new.
5. Firm redesign after interoperability changes government consent: Boom (2001), Ruiz (2004 working paper), Grossman–McCalman–Staiger (2021), JPET's own Theorem 3. A numerical threshold \(\eta_F^*<\eta_N^*\) is a reparameterized sign reversal until an independent economic condition is demonstrated.
6. National committee leadership correlates with exports, R&D or standards uptake: Mattli–Büthe (2003), Blind–von Laer (2022), Lessmann–Yao (2026), and Schmidt–Steingress (2022).

**Result:** zero candidate headline propositions pass the reviewer-first novelty gate. This is an affirmative screening result, not an assertion that no publication-worthy problem exists.

## 3. ONE substantive research frontier that survives as a QUESTION, not a claimed theorem

**When do formally approved interoperability standards fail to translate into effective, commercially realized interoperability because firms strategically adjust residual product characteristics or decline implementation—and when does national standard-setting anticipatorily correct or exacerbate this gap?**

Why the question matters: price competition and local/national standards decisions alone cannot tell standards organizations whether the published technical interface creates open competition, interoperable products or increased cross-border access. The actual outcome depends on firm design, tools, installed bases, implementation and market coverage. Standard-compliant products can remain incompatible (established observations). But the genuinely NEW part, if any, must be a nonadditive comparative static of national standard-setting governance WITH later strategic implementation.

**No desired direction is imposed.** The joint response might lead to standards that are too strong, too weak, delayed, or privately circumvented. Do not add a mandatory national "adjustment cost" to engineer an inversion. Do not confuse physical standardization scope, product similarity, and realized interoperability.

### Minimal theoretical structure to test, only after the next literature pass

1. One selected interface/scope s, interpreted as the objectively measurable breadth/strictness of shared interface obligations, not national tariffs, brand count or installed user count.
2. A plausible technical and economic implementation mechanism: firms individually choose whether to implement and/or how much private residual design to keep. Fixed investment, certification/conformance, complementary tooling, or patch costs must have identifiable economic primitives, not arbitrary country payments. Use AT MOST one such mechanism initially.
3. National standards bodies (not necessarily benevolent states) anticipate the post-standard choices and vote/suggest revisions according to a transparently specified national aggregation mechanism (domestic consumer and global firm exposure, or a defensible member-business objective).
4. Actual effectiveness is a downstream object: product-pair compatibility, market adoption/use, consumer access, or bilateral traded varieties. Do not treat ISO publication or national catalogue adoption as final effectiveness.
5. Solve a real policy choice, not mere 3-of-4 vote arithmetic; add a single committee rule only if it changes s and realized output.

### Mandatory constructive counterfactuals

- N0: freeze private post-publication design/adoption and compare committee proposals.
- N1: fix the technical scope as exogenous and compute post-publication firm adoption.
- N2: remove committee selection/governance, retaining firm adoption and technological primitives.
- N3: drop the putative institutional publication effect; if firm technologies and payoffs are the same, its unique continuation must be the same.
- N4: compare to the closest explicitly published propositions of Spulber 2019, Zhao-Xia-Shaw 2007, Boom 2001, GMS 2021, Klimenko 2009, and legal/engineering implementation research.

A viable new result must be **qualitative, economically large enough to matter**, and require a joint mechanism not surviving N0–N3 unchanged. It must be robust to an open parameter region or a simple condition, not a small constructed numeric reversal. If the result survives simple deletion of a mechanism, the deleted mechanism was not the scientific contribution.

## 4. Real-world testability: VERIFIED sources and limitations

### A. Machine-readable ISO metadata (public and primary)
https://www.iso.org/open-data.html (2026 source documentation):
- Deliverable dataset, daily updates, first published 2025-03-05: standard/reference identifier, date of publication, current development stage, replacement chain, edition, International Classification for Standards (ICS), committee and open project scope.
- Technical committees dataset, weekly updates, first published 2025-03-05: national standards body P-members/O-members, committee secretariat, scope, creation date.
- Neither table's documented fields include **nation-by-nation votes on individual DIS/FDIS**, firm-level certified implementation, actual installed base, cross-vendor interoperability tests or historical time series of committee member changes before dataset launch. Snapshots may be collected prospectively for membership changes; no past full-panel claim without separate archived records.
- **Note on licensing:** ISO Open Data is ODC-By; ISO's main proprietary standards *full texts* have separate restrictions. Do not scrape/copy ISO standard texts as if Open Data authorized doing so.

### B. Country-level adoption, two different outcomes
- ISO/IEC Guide 21-1:2005 describes national adoption and technical deviations: https://www.iso.org/cms/%20render/live/en/sites/isoorg/contents/data/standard/03/97/39799.html . National catalogue adoption is **not** automatically actual implementation by commercial firms.
- DIN catalogue ISO/EN ISO designation guidance: https://www.din.de/en/about-standards/din-standards/deliverables ; national adoption identifiable for some national databases but **cross-country availability/completeness and license have not been verified**.
- CEN-CENELEC official https://www.cencenelec.eu/european-standardization/ requires its members to publish adopted EN as their national standard, but actual industrial use generally remains voluntary. Hence nation-level formal EN adoption inside CEN is near mechanical; lack of a treatment/control adoption comparison matters.
- ISO DIS/FDIS individual votes belong to ISO's member-balloting system and are made available to member bodies; do not assume public vote-by-country histories exist. Official rules https://assets.iec.ch/sd/ISO-IEC_Directives_Part-1_2026.html .

### C. Outcomes and confounds
- WTO ePing TBT/SPS notification dataset + API, daily updates and classification in HS/ICS, https://data.wto.org/en/dataset/ext_eping ; public regulatory notifications, not ISO votes or actual firm adoption.
- WTO Trade Concerns https://tradeconcerns.wto.org/ ; governments' expressed market-access concerns, again different from ISO technical negative ballots.
- ISO-specific national standard adoption (selected domestic catalogs); trade via international HS product flows with **many-to-many ICS–HS crosswalk**, which is a research design problem not an automatic join.
- Sector-specific conformance test records, product registrations, firm standards use, compatibility plug-fests or certificates require a separate feasibility audit. Conformance to a standard must not be equated with successful product-pair interoperability.
- Import/export, pricing, entry, number of distinct product variants may provide outcomes, but simple trends around a standard publication are endogenous: standards respond to market needs, anticipation, R&D, market size, and institutional committee participation.

## 5. Evidence thresholds for reviewer clearance

**Literature (must pass):** precise nearest published proposition/assumptions and a counterexample or logical reason it does not imply proposed result; unavailable original proofs must remain flagged unresolved.

**Theory (must pass):** minimal fully specified game, real prices and firm best response, strategic adoption, truthful institutional timeline, national body objective and one economically interpretable theorem with an open-set/broad-condition domain. No fiat firm implementation or country damages, no mathematical reparameterization of an old sign result.

**Economics (must pass):** sharp observable prediction on effective interoperability, adoption, cross-border entry/trade, or strategic design — not just count of yes ballots or an arbitrary parameter root.

**Empirics (must pass):** one actual dataset/test protocol verifying both an exposure and the predicted response; causality claims held off until identification design and data matching are audited.

**Editorial GO:** only when both prior-art survival and substantive relevance are demonstrated. Until then: RESEARCH FRONTIER, not a recommended manuscript theorem or probability of journal acceptance.

## 7. Concrete and presently verified real-world technical families (added after official-source checks)

### ISO 15118: EV charging communication
- ISO 15118-20:2022 defines second-generation EV-to-EVSE application/network interface requirements. Official ISO metadata: https://committee.iso.org/cms/live/live/en/sites/isoorg/contents/data/standard/07/78/77845.html
- ISO/PAS 15118-23:2026 publicly describes **conformance** test cases and expressly states that actual downstream *interoperability* testing involves implementation application logic beyond that conformance document; an exhaustive test suite is practically impossible. Official ISO Online Browsing Platform: https://www.iso.org/obp/ui?_escaped_fragment_=iso%3Astd%3Aiso%3Apas%3A15118%3A-23%3Aed-1%3Av1%3Aen
- CharIN (2026) implementation guidance covers real ISO 15118-2 / ISO 15118-20 cross-generation TLS/certificate/protocol configuration failures, https://www.charin.global/technology/knowledge-base/
- California Energy Commission 2025 VOLTS project report describes May 2023 cross-vendor tests involving EV, EVSE and system providers; https://www.energy.ca.gov/publications/2025/vehicle-interoperability-testing-symposium-volts
- **Data caution:** having event descriptions and standards metadata does NOT mean raw pairwise interoperability success records are public or represent the market. Check public participant and outcome microdata first.
- **Theoretical application:** separate formal technical content, implementer conformance and successful cross-vendor functionality. Do not equate implementation errors with strategically profitable proprietary design differences; distinguish deliberate strategic design from engineering defects.

### ISO 20022: cross-border financial messaging
- BIS CPMI updated requirements, published 26 Feb 2026, explicitly states variability in ISO 20022 implementation may undermine potential cross-border payments benefits; revisions aim to clarify harmonized data requirements, with adoption flexibility until 2027. https://www.bis.org/publications/harmonised-iso-20022-data-requirements-enhancing-cross-border-payments-updated-report
- **Institutional caution:** payment networks' own mandates and central-bank requirements can make deployment non-voluntary, so ISO 20022 is not a generic clean example of unrestricted firm adoption. Distinguish public interface text from private payment-system rules and regulation.
- **Empirical outcome possibility (not yet data-verified):** implementation-profile convergence, message repair/rejection rates, delays and cross-border processing costs; need permission and reproducible data.

### Pilot decision
Use ISO 15118 as the **first candidate empirical feasibility pilot** for effective interoperability, because actual conformance/interoperability separation is expressly defined in the standard and public testing events are documented. ISO 20022 is a second domain to check external validity, not a combined pooled sample. No systematic causal estimate or new theorem is claimed by citing these cases.
